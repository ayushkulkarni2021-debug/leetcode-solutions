## Problem: Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/best-time-to-buy-and-sell-stock/

### Approach
Track the minimum price seen so far and the maximum profit obtainable at each day.

### Complexity
- Time: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes
Typical: [7,1,5,3,6,4] → 5. Edge: [7,6,4,3,1] → 0.
