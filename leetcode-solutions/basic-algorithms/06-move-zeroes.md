## Problem: Move Zeroes (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/move-zeroes/

### Approach
Move all non-zero elements forward while preserving their order, then fill the remaining positions with zeroes.

### Complexity
- Time: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes
Typical: [0,1,0,3,12] → [1,3,12,0,0]. Edge: [0] → [0].
